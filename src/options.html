RECENT TABS SWITCHER

Settings

A small, local-first switcher for people who keep more tabs than they intended.

Keyboard shortcuts

On Ubuntu, hold Alt and tap Q to cycle forward. Add Shift to cycle backward, then release Alt to switch. Chrome controls extension shortcuts, so you can remap both commands.

Privacy

Tab IDs are kept in Chrome’s temporary session storage solely to order your tabs. Settings remain on this device. Nothing is transmitted, sold, or used for advertising or analytics.
